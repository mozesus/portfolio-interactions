Narrate or pause briefly

While you're searching for an answer, keep the pro in the loop. Narrating briefly is almost always the better choice. Only pause if the lookup will take longer, or if you're looking at something you can't say out loud.

Narrate briefly

When the lookup will only take a few seconds.

"One moment, I'm just confirming that for you."

Pause for a brief moment of silence

Take a brief moment of silence if the search will take longer, or if you're viewing something you can't say out loud.

"There is going to be a brief moment of silence while I look into this for you, but I'm still here in case you need anything."
During a longer pause

Check back in every 30 to 45 seconds. A quick check-in lets the pro know you haven't disappeared, even if you don't have an answer yet.

"Still here with you, [name], and working on this for you."
