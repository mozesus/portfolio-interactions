Resolve a pro's billing dispute

A pro says he paid for junk leads and wants his money back. Practice the full contact in a mock Lead Source support tool: verify him, check the record, apply credit policy, fix the root cause and close it out.

The scenario: You're a Lead Source billing support rep. Marcus Bell, owner of Bell & Sons Plumbing, opens a chat about $187 in lead charges this week. He says two leads were junk. One of them qualifies for a credit. One doesn't. Your job is to figure out which is which.
