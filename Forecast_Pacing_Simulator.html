You may hear pros ask why their leads came in faster some months than others. Three concepts explain why. Select each term below to learn what it means, then use the simulator to see how they interact.

Term 1
Spend Target
Select to learn more
The pro's monthly lead budget — the full value of leads available to them, not the amount they are billed.
Term 2
Forecast
Select to learn more
Lead Source's estimate of homeowner demand associated with the pro's tasks and coverage area. This shapes how many leads the pro is likely to receive.
Term 3
Pacing
Select to learn more
Leads are spread across the month and shared among similar pros — not sent all at once. Pacing helps distribute homeowner demand evenly through the billing cycle.
