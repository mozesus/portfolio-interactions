Scenario
"I just got charged for a lead but I still have budget left this month. What happened?"
In your own words, how would you explain this charge to the pro?

Write your response as if you are speaking directly to the pro.

Model response

Here is an example of a clear, accurate explanation. Key elements are highlighted.

Rep
"I completely understand why that would be confusing. This charge is for a Boost — a separate type of lead that is billed differently from your Subscription. Your monthly Subscription payment covers the leads you receive when homeowners select you up to your budget. Boosts are billed in real time when you accept them — they are not covered by your pre-paid Subscription budget. The Subscription discount also does not apply to Boosts, so they are priced separately. Would you like me to pull up the specific Boost on your account so I can walk you through it?"
